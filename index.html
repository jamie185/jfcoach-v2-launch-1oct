What changed,
and how we tested it

Eight testers each played a different client, three rounds in two days, and scored one question: is it as easy as opening Instagram?

Round 1 · 27 Sep

3.8 / 10

"I wouldn't know what to do tomorrow." Six tasks on Home, no first step.

Round 2 · 27 Sep night

5.0 / 10

One next move on Home. Setup still slow, screens disagreed on dates.

Round 3 · 28 Sep

6.0 / 10

"Opening the app and doing the first thing is easy." Scores 5 to 7.

Top fixes

  • Home leads with one next move from JFI, a short list and tomorrow's plan.
  • Setup went from about 6 minutes to 2. Setup, Home and Training agree on the first workout and start date.
  • Injury is asked before any program is built. If yes, a person sets it up.
  • Short on time: a 20 minute version that counts, move to a free day, or already done.
  • Food opens on your next meal. Tomorrow is clearly tomorrow. Readable shopping list and recipe search.
  • Logging a workout: swaps keep your sets, done exercises fold away.
  • Challenge folded to one screen. Games, the map and measurements no longer crash.
  • The program builder counts your PT days and gives beginners gentler advice.
  • One place each for habits, messages, settings and notifications.

Still being fixed this week

  • A few counts don't match everywhere yet, for example a logged recipe or workout points showing on one screen but not on Home.
  • "What's tomorrow" should read the same on Home, JFI and the calendar.
  • A saved, half-finished workout should resume from Home.
  • Food still feels like calorie counting to some testers (see decision 7).

Testers used a practice copy with sample data, so some of what they hit only happens there. Every fix is checked again before go-live.

Jamie's calls
before 1 Oct

Product and marketing only. Everything else is being handled.

Announce the prizes

The site promised prizes "before 1 October". The only prize named in the app is 12 weeks of free PT. What do the Champion and each path winner get?

Weekly challenge: 100 or 150 points

The website says +100. The app gives 150 when the week matches your path, 100 otherwise. Keep 150 and fix the site, or make it 100 for everyone?

4 paths or 5

Setup offers five: Strong, Tone, Trim, Fit and Wellbeing. Some copy says four. Which is it?

17 gyms or 18

The page says real trainers at 18 Melbourne gyms, the brief says 17. Which number goes out on 1 Oct?

What the leaderboard ranks

The board ranks by points. The Champion is picked after week 6 by the 60/30/10 rule, and the board says so. Keep it, or rank by the champion rule all along?

JF+ sign-up link

Prize winners need JF+, but there is no JF+ or 3-session pack checkout link in the app yet. Which links should it use?

Calories on meal cards

Every meal card shows its calories. Keep them (Gabrielle's view and the design so far), or hide them for clients who hate counting?

Tomorrow's meals

Tomorrow is look-only so clients can plan the shop. Should they be able to tick meals ahead?

Earliest daily plan: 7am

Nothing goes out before 7am, so a 6am trainer gets the plan after their session. Keep 7am, or allow earlier?

Habit reminders on Quiet and Light

Right now Quiet and Light get no habit reminders. Is that right, or should Light keep one?

A person for clients with no trainer

Injury notes and "make it lighter" requests go to the client's trainer. Who gets them when there's no trainer? Gabrielle is the suggestion.

Safety routing on

When a client tells JFI about an injury, feeling unwell or cancelling, JFI stops coaching that and passes it to a person. Turn it on before the pushes start? Recommended.
